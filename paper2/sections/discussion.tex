\subsection{Threats to validity}
One machine and a compressed surge, as in the first
paper~\cite{pathak2026surviving}; a modelled portal cost; a student model
whose parameters come from a lab study of a mock result, in which students
are likely calmer than on a real result day; bots modelled as simple
scrapers, not adversaries that forge tickets or open many sessions. The room
has its own budget (0.25 CPU), and in a pilot on a smaller machine it ran out
of CPU and memory at 5$\times$ before the backend did; we report runs in
which the room was at its CPU limit and vary its budget
(\texttt{p2-roomcpu}).
